## Scope

- Excludes Team Rocket by trainer class/pic/music containing `rocket`.
- Keeps gym leaders, Elite Four, champions, Red, Silver, and Eusine out of automated swap suggestions.
- Ignores trainer references from Emerald map groups, Kanto maps, and SS Anne/ship maps before counting.
- Counts only enabled, non-legendary, non-mythical, non-battle-only-form species for coverage totals.
- Uses `src/data/wild_encounters.json` as the level sanity check for candidate additions.
