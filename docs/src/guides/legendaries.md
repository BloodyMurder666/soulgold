### Legendaries
- Zapdos: Olivine Lighthouse top, after 8th badge
- Articuno: Snowtop Mountain (Rock climb required)
- Moltres: Victory Road
- Mewtwo: Nameless Dungeon in Kitakami Border, after Elite Four, requires rock climb
- Mew: After obtaining 8 badges. Visit Olivine City's port to find Blaine. Fight him to unlock access to Faraway Island where Mew can be found.
- Raikou: Roaming after Burned Tower event
- Entei: Roaming after Burned Tower event
- Suicune: Roaming after Burned Tower event
- Ho-Oh: Choose Rainbow Wing after Rocket Takeover, if you chose Silver Wing, talk to director after Elite Four, prepare for a fight
- Lugia: Choose Silver Wing after Rocket Takeover, if you chose Rainbow Wing, talk to director after Elite four, prepare for a fight
- Shadow Lugia: Explore south of Johto to find Abandoned Rocket Warehouse. Solve the puzzles inside to find a Dark Crystal. Bring it to regular Lugia Encounter. If you have already caught regular lugia, do one round of title defense to reset the spawn. If you picked Ho-Oh during the main story, then fight the Director in the postgame to obtain Silver Wing.
- Celebi: Solve all 8 Ruins of Alph puzzles (4 slider puzzles and 4 wall puzzles), then enter a passage in B1F to find the GS Ball. Take it to Kurt in Azalea, then go to the Ilex Forest shrine.

[spoiler=Celebi wall puzzle solutions]
- ESCAPE: Use an Escape Rope from the Bag.
- WATER: Use a Water Stone from the Bag.
- LIGHT: Use Flash from the party menu.
- HOOH: Bring Ho-Oh with you.
[/spoiler]

- Regirock: Solve the braille puzzle in Vajra Desert West.

[spoiler=Regirock puzzle solution]
Bring Tyranitar to the wall.
[/spoiler]

- Regice: Solve the braille puzzle in Snowtop Mountain.

[spoiler=Regice puzzle solution]
Bring Baxcalibur to the wall.
[/spoiler]

- Registeel: Solve the braille puzzle in Railway Cave.

[spoiler=Registeel puzzle solution]
Bring Kommo-o to the wall.
[/spoiler]
- Latias: Vajra Desert East Temple in postgame 
- Latios: Vajra Desert East Temple in postgame
- Rayquaza: Defeat Steven after becoming Champion (Kitakami house), then go to Embedded Tower
- Jirachi: Finish rival story in Mt. Silver
- Regigigas: Bring all three Regis to Mt Silver backroom
- Heatran: Explore Mt. Mortar Depths after obtaining Waterfall and Rock Climb.
- Uxie: Route 30 Rock Climb spot near the Berry man house.
- Mesprit: Route 47 Rock Climb spot near the last bridge before Route 48.
- Azelf: Mahogany Town Rock Climb spot.

[spoiler=Heatran puzzle solution]
Select the lower line and the middle top.
[/spoiler]
- Phione/Manaphy: Fish with Good or Super rod in South Johto Sea area where only Shellos and Gastrodon can be encountered. Evolves to Manaphy at level 58.
- Cresselia: After finishing rival story in Mt Silver. Talk to them in their house to start the quest. Go to Lake of Rage and speak with Hidden Power Dude, then explore the north-east corner of the lake.
- Darkrai: After obtaining Cresselia, show it to your rival in their house. Then go to Cherrygrove and enter the inn with Cresselia in your party.

[spoiler=Darkrai inn puzzle solution]
1. Accept the Moon Key.
2. Go to your room at the top of the stairs, examine the bed, and choose not to sleep/resist sleeping.
3. Go outside, return to the lobby, and inspect the papers on the wall.
4. Inspect the Luxray trophy in the long hallway.
5. Inspect the old telephone on the front desk and call the number formed by the stack of papers.
6. Examine the piano to find a small key, then use it on the nearby safe.
7. Walk upstairs to find Darkrai.
[/spoiler]
- Shaymin: Dream Garden, accessible from Tohjo Falls after obtaining Rock Climb
- Victini: After becoming champion, speak with old lady in Kitakami Border house and defeat all gym leader rematches
- Virizion: Route 50
- Cobalion: Railway Cave Route 33 side exit after becoming a champion
- Terrakion: Dark Cave North after becoming a champion
- Magerna: After becoming champion, enter Rocket Arcade and speak with the burglar. Win 25 rounds of Arcade and speak with him again. He will give you a key to Goldenrod Apartment top floor. Prepare for a fight.
- Original Color Magerna: obtain 100 trophies
- Marshadow: After 8th badge, return to Elder Li in the sprout tower. Prepare for a battle. Then explore Sprout tower basement.
- Poipole: Obtain 45 trophies
- Meltan: Trade for Tinkaton in Rinto Village
- Kubfu: Found in the Rock Climb cave in Blackthorn City. Scrolls used to evolve it can be bought from the Rocket Arcade for a very high price.

[spoiler=Kubfu cave solution]
Go down, up, left, up, left, down, right, right.
[/spoiler]
- Zarude: Obtain 75 trophies
- Hoopa: Requires having completed the Rinto Village trade for giving Tinkaton to the scientist in the bottom house of the village. After becoming a Champion, go to Vajra pyramid and explore the depths. Prepare for multiple battles.
- Bloodmoon Ursaluna: Explore Kitakami Mountain to find the Black Mirror, then go to the Lost Woods.

[spoiler=Bloodmoon Ursaluna route solution]
Follow the Murkrow.
[/spoiler]
- Ogerpon: Explore Kitakami Mountain
- Chien Pao: After obtaining Waterfall and Rock Climb, explore Ice Path further
- Fezandipiti: After obtaining Rock Climb, explore Kitakami
- Paradox Pokémon: Explore South Johto Meteor Island
- Paradox Entei/Raikou/Suicune: Register all past Paradox Pokemon in the Pokedex, then interact with the Unown wall in the back.

[spoiler=Past Paradox wall puzzle solution]
In the next room, bring Entei, Raikou, and Suicune to their respective walls to open them.
[/spoiler]

- Paradox Virizion/Cobalion/Terrakion: Register all future Paradox Pokemon in the Pokedex, then interact with the wall in the back.

[spoiler=Future Paradox wall puzzle solution]
Bring Virizion, Terrakion, and Cobalion to their respective walls to unlock them.
[/spoiler]
- Eternal Floette: Obtain 60 trophies
- Zeraora: Accumulate 30 winstreak in Battle Tower to spawn Zeraora outside the tower.
- Meloetta: After becoming a Champion, you can find Meloetta in the Ecruteak Dance Theater.
